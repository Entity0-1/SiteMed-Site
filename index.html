What it checks

A practical checkup for the page in front of you.

Results are based on evidence SiteMed can observe in Chrome. Technical details stay available inside an optional advanced explanation.

01

Things that look broken

Missing images and media, links that lead nowhere, cut-off text, controls outside the window, and visible page errors.

02

Forms and accessibility

Unclear form boxes, unnamed buttons, missing image descriptions, page language, heading problems, and unsafe form behavior.

03

Search and loading

Page titles and descriptions, search visibility settings, mobile setup, slow content, delayed display, and unexpected layout movement.

04

Strong trust warnings

Insecure pages, exposed password or payment forms, and unusual form destinations. SiteMed reports evidence without declaring a site safe.

Good to know

Clear limits make a better diagnosis.

Can SiteMed guarantee that a website is safe?

No. SiteMed points out strong warning signs visible on the current page. It does not use a reputation database and cannot prove that a website or seller is trustworthy.

Does SiteMed repair websites?

SiteMed explains what it found, who can most likely fix it, and what to try next. It does not change the website itself.

Why can two scans look different?

Results can change with the browser window, connection, cached files, and content loaded by the website after the page opens.

Where is saved information kept?

Saved scans, audits, monitoring results, notes, and report branding stay in Chrome extension storage on the user’s device.
